## Overheating
Possible causes:
- Cooling system blockage
- High ambient temperature
- Internal battery cell fault
- Poor thermal management

Recommended actions:
- Inspect the cooling system
- Check battery temperature history
- Inspect battery cells for abnormal behavior
- Verify thermal sensors

## Voltage Drop
Possible causes:
- Weak cells
- Loose electrical connection
- Battery aging

Recommended actions:
- Check battery voltage
- Inspect connectors
- Perform a battery health test
